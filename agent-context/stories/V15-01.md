# V15-01 — Grove hardware identity and wiring contract

- **Status:** Done
- **Priority:** P0
- **Objective:** Record enough physical evidence and electrical constraints to build, but not yet
  flash, the first AtmosMesh Grove v1.5 image safely.

## Scope

- Record the controller probe, product identity, wired digital sensors and display geometry.
- Make the D3/GPIO0 boot-strap risk explicit.
- Separate confirmed wiring from the DHT11 data-pin assumption.

## Out of scope

- Flashing or replacing the working AT firmware.
- YL-69/YL-38, LDR, MAX4466, networking, MQTT or calibration.
- Any change to the ESP32 station's electrical rules.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] ESP8266EX, 26 MHz crystal and 4 MB flash are recorded from a read-only probe.
- [x] Product name, variant and ID-based default identity are accepted.
- [x] OLED/BMP180 I²C and DHT11 profile pins are recorded with evidence quality.
- [x] Every fitted module is specified at 3.3 V and GPIO0 boot behavior is documented.
- [x] Flashing remains an explicit operator gate.

## Validation plan

Compare the decision log, inventory and firmware README for identical pin/voltage/identity facts.
No energized or destructive hardware action is part of this validation.

## Evidence

- 2026-08-23 read-only session: ROM loader responded; ESP8266EX, 26 MHz crystal and 4 MB flash
  detected; installed AT firmware answered `AT` with `OK`; nothing erased or flashed.
- 2026-08-24 operator: OLED, BMP180 and DHT11 wired; OLED SDA=D2/GPIO4 and SCL=D3/GPIO0.
- D-008 through D-010 and `docs/hardware/inventory.md` distinguish confirmed facts from the
  DHT11 DATA=D5/GPIO14 profile assumption.

## Residual risk

The exact board model/schematic and DHT11 data joint have not been photographed. Runtime sensor
validation therefore belongs to V15-03 after explicit flash authorization.
