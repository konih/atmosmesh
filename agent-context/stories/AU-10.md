# AU-10 — 48-hour unattended run, then hand it over

- **Status:** Ready
- **Priority:** P0
- **Milestone:** Aura v1
- **Depends on:** AU-01 … AU-09

## User story

As the operator, I want to watch the thing survive two days of nothing happening before I give it
to someone who cannot debug it.

## Outcome

A gift has no operator. Every failure it can have, it must have already had on my bench.

## What gets done to it

| Event | Expected behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| power cut, repeated | boots to Now; Wi-Fi, place and VOC baseline all survive |
| Wi-Fi router off for an hour | sensors keep rendering; outdoor strip disappears; rejoins alone |
| sensor pigtail pulled | `FAULT`, then clean recovery when replugged |
| left untouched 48 h | no memory growth, no watchdog reset, no frozen reading |
| touched repeatedly | no drift, no stuck screen, no lost calibration |

## Acceptance criteria

1. 48 hours with no reboot that was not deliberately caused.
2. Free heap at hour 48 within 5 % of hour 1 — the leak test.
3. Every state from AU-03 observed at least once during the run.
4. A one-page card for the recipient: what it measures, what the words mean, how to change the
   Wi-Fi, and what *not* to worry about.
5. No `co2` or `ppm` anywhere in the shipped image.

## The handover card matters

The recipient did not ask for a VOC Index. If the card does not explain in four sentences why 100
is normal and 250 means open a window, the number is decoration.
