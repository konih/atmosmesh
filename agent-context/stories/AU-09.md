# AU-09 — Enclosure and the self-heating number

- **Status:** Ready
- **Priority:** P1
- **Milestone:** Aura v1
- **Depends on:** AU-06

## User story

As the recipient, I want the temperature on the screen to be the temperature of my room, so that
I can trust the one number I am most likely to check against my own senses.

## Outcome

A measured offset, or a placement that removes the need for one. §4.1 of the design calls an
uncorrected self-heating error *"a broken gift"*, and it is the risk most likely to survive all
the way to someone's shelf unnoticed.

**Dropping the ENS160 already removed most of this risk.** That module's AHT20 sat beside its own
hotplate, and it was the compensation source. With the BME280 on a pigtail and one small SGP41
heater, the remaining sources are the ESP32, the TFT driver and the backlight — all on the board,
none touching the sensor.

## Method

1. Run ≥ 30 min from cold in still air until the reading stops rising.
2. Compare BME280 against a reference thermometer in the same air.
3. Record the steady-state delta in `inventory.md` with the enclosure it was measured in.
4. If the delta exceeds 1.0 °C, fix it with placement or ventilation — **not** with a fudge
   constant in firmware. A constant offset is a lie that tracks nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A number exists, with the enclosure and ambient conditions it was measured under.
2. The offset is < 1.0 °C, or the enclosure changed until it was.
3. No hard-coded temperature correction exists in the config.
4. Backlight brightness is shown not to move the reading materially.
