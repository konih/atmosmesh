# R2-01 — AtmosMesh Room v2: sensor set, bus map and power domains

- **Status:** Proposed (design spec; no hardware touched)
- **Priority:** P2
- **Milestone:** Room v2 design
- **Depends on:** photo identification of every module below (the R2 equivalent of RLS-01);
  the 4 September 2026 stock pass in `docs/elektronik-inventar.md`

## User story

As the operator, I want a second Room-class station that measures real CO₂ and particulates with
sensors designed for those quantities, and that reports presence rather than motion, so that the
first Room stays sealed in its housing as it is and the new unit answers the questions the first
one cannot.

## Outcome

A perfboard carrier and an `atmosmesh-room-v2` firmware product with one 3.3 V I²C bus carrying
SCD41, SPS30, ENS160+AHT20, VEML7700 and SHT41, a radar presence input, the existing buzzer stage,
and a single protected 5 V domain that feeds only the SPS30 (and the radar if its datasheet demands
5 V). Every value reaches Home Assistant over the existing MQTT contract shape with unit, validity
and age; CO₂ carries a calibration state.

## Why a second unit instead of extending Room

- Room is soldered, housed and running. Adding sensors reopens its wiring gate and its enclosure.
- There is one SCD41 and one SPS30. They belong in the unit that is designed around them.
- Room's hardest problems — the SDS011's 5 V UART, the laser lifetime (ROOM-04), the PIR's motion
  vs presence gap (ROOM-05) — disappear when the sensors change. Carrying that protection circuitry
  into a design that no longer needs it would be waste.

## Sensor set and I²C address map

All on one bus, 3.3 V, 100 kHz for commissioning. No two devices share an address:

| Device | Address | Measures | Supply | Notes (type knowledge until photographed) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sensirion SCD41 | `0x62` | CO₂ ppm, T, RH | 3.3 V sensor rail | Photoacoustic NDIR. 2.4–5.5 V. Draws short bursts up to ~200 mA during measurement; needs local bulk decoupling. 5 s periodic mode. Publish with calibration state; decide ASC vs forced calibration in R2-05 |
| Sensirion SPS30 | `0x69` | PM1, PM2.5, PM4, PM10 µg/m³, number concentrations | **5 V domain** | 4.5–5.5 V supply, ~60 mA average, ~80 mA peak. `SEL` pin to GND selects I²C. Interface pins are specified for 3.3 V logic — **confirm from the datasheet before wiring**, and add the PDF to `docs/hardware/datasheets/`. Built-in fan auto-clean and sleep; no laser-lifetime duty cycling needed. Connector is JST ZHR-5, 1.5 mm pitch: the JST-PH set in stock is 2.0 mm and will not fit |
| ENS160 + AHT20 | `0x52`/`0x53` + `0x38` | TVOC ppb, AQI index, eCO₂ (VOC-derived) | 3.3 V sensor rail | Module has its own regulator for the 1.8 V core. eCO₂ is published as `voc_eco2`, never `co2`. Alternative is the SGP41 at `0x59`; choose one, not both |
| Sensirion SHT41 | `0x44` | T, RH reference | 3.3 V sensor rail | Independent of the SCD41's self-heating-compensated T/RH. Both are published, named by source |
| Vishay VEML7700 | `0x10` | lux | 3.3 V sensor rail | Same connector contract as Room (`VIN, 3Vo, GND, SCL, SDA`) |
| TI INA226 (CJMCU-226) | `0x40` | 5 V domain current and voltage | 3.3 V sensor rail | High-side on the 5 V feed to the SPS30. Fit for commissioning; may stay populated. Read the shunt value off the board |
| 0.96" OLED (fallback only) | `0x3C` | local status | 3.3 V | Only if the controller is a DevKit without TFT |

Bus load: with six to seven devices and the SPS30 on a cable, bus capacitance will be well above
Room's assumed 200 pF. Measure the rise time with the DSO2D15 at first power-up and adjust the
pull-ups from the measurement, not from the assumption. Breakout pull-ups: photograph each module
and sum them; the parallel total must stay at or above ~1.1 kΩ (3 mA at 3.3 V). SPS30 has none of
its own.

## Presence

- **HLK-LD2450** on UART2 (GPIO16/17), powered from the 5 V domain. It is the multi-target
  tracking radar: 5 V supply, 3.3 V UART logic (confirm from the datasheet), a continuous frame
  stream at 256000 baud with up to three targets' coordinates, and **no `OUT` pin**. Presence is
  derived in firmware from "any target with non-zero coordinates", with a hold time.
- The LD2410S (the 3.3 V, low-power member of the family, with an `OUT` pin) went to
  [SP-01](SP-01.md), whose board has no 5 V domain and cannot run the LD2450. Room v2 has a
  5 V domain and a UART, so it takes the radar that needs both. The earlier plan of `OUT` first
  and UART later therefore no longer applies: R2-03 implements the LD2450 frame decoder before
  presence exists on this unit.
- A second LD2410S is cheap; if one is bought, the `OUT`-pin plan in the Room wiring can be
  restored and the LD2450 becomes the spare again.

## Power domains

```text
5 V in (USB now; enclosed 230 V → 5 V 5 A PSU later, with the 1 A fuse holder on its mains side;
        external input through a 1N5822 reverse-polarity Schottky, USB not)
 ├─ controller VIN/5V ──► controller LDO ──► 3V3_CTRL: ESP32, TFT, buzzer load
 ├─ AMS1117-3.3 (own part, 22 µF tantalum/electrolytic out) ──► 3V3_SENS: SCD41, SHT41, VEML7700,
 │                                          ENS160+AHT20, INA226 logic, I²C pull-ups (100 µF bulk at SCD41)
 └─ RXEF050 PPTC (0.5 A hold) ──► [optional PNP high-side gate, GPIO27] ──► INA226 shunt ──► SPS30 VDD
                                                                              (+ LD2450 radar, + optional fan)
```

Every I²C device and both pull-ups sit on `3V3_SENS` so that no partial-power state can push
current into an unpowered sensor through its protection diodes; the INA226's logic side moved
there for that reason. The drawing set — board zone map on the 31 × 27 grid, power tree, bus
tree and one connection diagram per module — is in
[`hardware/kicad/atmosmesh-room-v2/wiring.md`](../../hardware/kicad/atmosmesh-room-v2/wiring.md)
with [`atmosmesh-room-v2-zones.svg`](../../hardware/kicad/atmosmesh-room-v2/atmosmesh-room-v2-zones.svg).

Three small circuits there use the 4 September diode and MOSFET stock: the buzzer is switched by
an IRLB8721 low-side driver so GPIO25 sources only gate charge; an optional S8050 + S8550 high-side
gate lets firmware power-cycle the SPS30 domain (high-side, so the sensor's I²C reference never
floats); and an optional IRLB8721 + 1N5819 low-side output on GPIO26 drives a small enclosure fan
against the ENV-01 still-air problem.

- **Separate sensor 3.3 V regulator.** The SCD41's measurement bursts on top of ESP32 Wi-Fi peaks
  exceed what `hardware/power.md` allows on a devboard LDO. One of the four AMS1117-3.3 in stock,
  on copper, carries the sensor rail; the controller LDO carries only what it did on Room.
- **5 V domain feeds one consumer.** No 5 V ever reaches the I²C bus. The PPTC hold current (0.5 A)
  sits above the SPS30 fan start-up and below anything that would cook a trace.
- **Rail measurement stays manual for 3.3 V** (D-032), but the 5 V domain gets the INA226 because
  that is the rail whose current the design actually argues about.

## MQTT contract additions

Same envelope as Room (`{value, unit, valid, age_ms}`, `id`, `device`); new product
`atmosmesh-room-v2`, station id `atmosmesh-0003` or next free.

| Entity | Unit | Source | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `co2` | ppm | SCD41 only | Carries `calibration` attribute: `factory`, `asc`, `frc` |
| `pm1`, `pm2_5`, `pm4`, `pm10` | µg/m³ | SPS30 | Invalid while fan is in clean or start-up |
| `tvoc` | ppb | ENS160 | Invalid during warm-up; `warmup` attribute |
| `aqi` | index | ENS160 | UBA index 1–5 |
| `voc_eco2` | ppm | ENS160 | Never named `co2`; never in a CO₂ card |
| `temperature_scd41`, `temperature_sht41`, `humidity_*` | °C, % | both | Named by source |
| `lux` | lx | VEML7700 | As Room |
| `presence` | binary | LD2450 UART frames | Any target reported, with a hold time; `targets` count as an attribute. Verified with a person sitting still before it is trusted (ROOM-05 lesson) |
| `fan` | switch | `J_AUX` via GPIO26 | Command topic plus state; `co2`, `temperature_*` and `humidity_*` are `valid: false` while it runs and for a settling time after |

## In scope

- Perfboard carrier generated with the Room tooling (`generate_project.py`, validators), new pad
  map, BOM from stock.
- New composition root `firmware/src/products/atmosmesh_room_v2.cpp` per ADR-0001; host-tested
  SCD4x and SPS30 frame decoders (both use the Sensirion CRC-8 already implemented for the SHT41);
  ENS160 driver; radar `OUT` input.
- Staged bring-up: 3.3 V sensors first, then the 5 V domain with the INA226 reading, then radar.
- Enclosure fan: the small axial fan in stock (marking dictated as "EXAV-XV-B0", rating still to
  be read) on the `J_AUX` output, pulsed for a few seconds before each measurement so the SCD41
  and SHT41 sample moving air. Firmware exposes `fan` as a switch entity and marks `co2` and the
  temperatures invalid while the fan runs.
- Enclosure notes: SPS30 inlet and outlet must reach outside air; SCD41 away from the regulator
  and TFT backlight (ENV-01 lesson); radar with a clear field of view.

## Out of scope

- Any change to the running Room, Grove or Aqua units.
- ESP32-C3 / C6 boards as the Room v2 controller. The C3 toolchain itself is proven since
  4 September (`firmware/tools/c3scan` builds; see SP-01), but the ideaspark board stays the v2
  controller for pad-map reuse.
- Radar tracking beyond presence: target coordinates and zones stay out until presence is proven.
- Claiming SCD41 accuracy without the fresh-air and occupancy evidence from R2-05.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given every module in the table above, when photographed front and back, then supply,
      pull-ups, connector order and the SPS30 logic level are recorded in `docs/hardware/inventory.md`.
- [ ] Given the carrier plan, when `validate_room_carrier.py` and the perfboard validator run,
      then no net touches GPIO1, 2, 3, 4, 12, 15, 18, 23 or 32 and no 5 V net reaches an I²C pad.
- [ ] Given the sensor rail energised alone, when the bus is scanned, then exactly the addresses
      `0x10`, `0x38`, `0x44`, `0x52` or `0x53`, `0x62` (and `0x3C` if OLED) answer.
- [ ] Given the 5 V domain energised, when the SPS30 starts, then the INA226 logs the start-up
      peak and the PPTC does not trip.
- [ ] Given fresh outdoor air, when the SCD41 has run for 30 minutes, then `co2` reads 400–450 ppm
      with `calibration` stated; given a closed occupied room, the value rises and later falls.
- [ ] Given a person sitting still for 5 minutes, when `presence` is read, then it stays `on`;
      given the room empty for 5 minutes, it is `off`.

## Validation

- Automated: frame decoders and CRC against Sensirion test vectors; MQTT payload schema; invalid
  states during warm-up and fan clean.
- Manual: bus scan, rise-time capture, INA226 start-up log, CO₂ plausibility, presence hold.
- Failure/edge case: SPS30 unplugged (bus must keep working); SCD41 burst during Wi-Fi TX (no
  brown-out); radar `OUT` stuck high.

## Open questions (block the carrier, not the firmware)

1. Is the second ideaspark board the TFT or the OLED variant? Decides controller and pad map.
2. SPS30 cable: source a JST ZH 1.5 mm pigtail or solder to the pads.
3. LD2450 supply current and UART logic level from its datasheet; the current sets whether the
   RXEF050 still holds with SPS30, fan and radar on one domain.
4. Which SCD41 breakout is in stock (pull-ups fitted or not)?
5. Which 5 V source for the first bench run: USB or the bench supply through `JP_5V_SRC`?
6. The fan's label: rated voltage, frame size, current and lead count. Only a 5 V two-wire fan
   goes on `J_AUX`; a 12 V fan needs its own supply and the fan output stays empty.
