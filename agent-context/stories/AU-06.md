# AU-06 — Live sensors on screen, with honest failure

- **Status:** Ready
- **Priority:** P0
- **Milestone:** Aura v1
- **Depends on:** AU-02, AU-03, AU-04

## User story

As the recipient, I want the numbers on screen to be the numbers the sensors just measured, and
to be told plainly when they are not.

## Outcome

Five channels bound to the Now screen, with AU-03's state machine between sensor and pixel:

| Channel | Source | Interval | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VOC Index | SGP41 | 5 s | the hero number; `WARMING` for 90 samples |
| NOx Index | SGP41 | 5 s | secondary row; needs a longer learning window than VOC |
| Temperature | BME280 | 5 s | the displayed room temperature |
| Humidity | BME280 | 5 s | also the SGP41's compensation source |
| Pressure | BME280 | 30 s | slow-moving; no need to redraw often |

**Compensation is wired, not decorative.** The SGP41's `compensation:` block takes the BME280's
humidity and temperature. Without it the algorithm assumes 50 %RH / 25 °C and the index drifts
with the weather rather than with the air.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Unplugging the pigtail mid-run shows `FAULT` within one interval; values do not freeze.
2. Reconnecting recovers without a reboot — ESPHome's bus recovery is relied on and observed.
3. The SGP41 never displays `0` during stabilisation.
4. `store_baseline: true` is set, and the VOC Index after a power cycle resumes near its learned
   baseline rather than restarting the 12-hour learning window.
5. A soak of ten minutes shows no reading older than 3 × its interval while healthy.
