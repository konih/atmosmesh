# SP-01 — AtmosMesh Spot: a 3.3 V presence-and-climate node on an ESP32-C3 SuperMini

- **Status:** Proposed (design spec; carrier soldering started 4 September 2026)
- **Priority:** P3 — small, self-contained, and the ESP32-C3 toolchain spike R2-01 needs anyway
- **Milestone:** Spot v1
- **Depends on:** nothing open. SuperMini pin map and OLED bus pins are photo- and
  scan-verified, the LD2410S datasheet is read, the SHT41 and VEML7700 breakouts are photographed
  (see Evidence).
- **First unit:** SHT41 + VEML7700 + HLK-LD2410S on the board and the DS18B20 probe on a
  terminal. **No BME280** (operator decision, 5 September 2026; the outdoor-cable variant that
  existed on 4 September is withdrawn). Drawings and bill of materials in
  [`hardware/kicad/atmosmesh-spot/wiring.md`](../../hardware/kicad/atmosmesh-spot/wiring.md).

## User story

As the operator, I want a palm-sized station that I can build in an evening, put in any room that
has a USB socket, and read on its own little screen or in Home Assistant, so that the rooms without
a Room-class station get temperature, humidity, light and — the thing PIR never gave me — whether
somebody is actually in the room; and I want a temperature probe on a lead so the same node can
watch a radiator, a window pane or the air outside a window without any electronics leaving the
room.

## Outcome

One carrier on a 14 × 20 hole perfboard, one population:

| Product id | On the board | On a terminal | Bus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `atmosmesh-spot` | SuperMini, SHT41 at `0x44`, VEML7700 at `0x10`, HLK-LD2410S radar | DS18B20 probe, 2.5 m, 1-Wire | one 3.3 V I²C bus at 100 kHz, OLED at `0x3C` |

Everything is one 3.3 V domain. Firmware is one product built with a PlatformIO `esp32-c3`
environment, publishing on the existing MQTT contract, with the on-board 72 × 40 OLED cycling
through the values.

**The first unit takes the last SHT41 spare and the only LD2410S.** After Room, Room v2 and
this unit, one SHT41 and one VEML7700 remain; a second Spot needs another SHT41 bought and a
second radar, or goes without presence. The six BME280 in stock stay unassigned.

## Sensor choices, and why

- **SHT41 for temperature and humidity.** The accurate one (about ±1.8 %RH, ±0.2 °C), and the
  last free spare.
- **No BME280.** Decided against on 5 September. It would have brought pressure and, on a cable,
  an outdoor reading; the operator prefers a simpler board without the cable, its series
  resistors, its clamps and its terminal. Pressure is not a Spot value; an outdoor temperature,
  if wanted, comes from the DS18B20 probe held out of the window.
- **VEML7700** for lux, as on Room. Mounted on its header facing the room, on the side away from
  the OLED so the display's own glow does not become the reading.
- **HLK-LD2410S** for presence. It is the 3.3 V, low-power member of the LD24xx family with a
  presence output pin and a UART, which is exactly what a board without a 5 V domain needs.
  There is one, and it was reserved for Room v2; Room v2 has a 5 V domain and takes the LD2450
  instead (R2-01 updated). The manual (read 4 September, filed under
  `docs/hardware/datasheets/`) settles it: 3.0–3.6 V supply, every pin 0–3.3 V, average current
  under 1 mA with transmit peaks near 118 mA, presence on pin `OT2` (high = occupied), UART on
  `OT1` (TX) and `RX` at 115200 8N1.
- **DS18B20 probe** because 1-Wire is built for a lead and the probe is already encapsulated.
  It is the only thing that may leave the room.
- **Not potted, not outside.** Coating the ceramic antenna detunes the SuperMini's weakest part.
- **Soil header dropped.** Three sensors and a radar fill the small board; a plant node is a
  different population and can be a later story.

## Controller: ESP32-C3 SuperMini OLED — verified

- 3.3 V logic and a small on-board LDO from USB 5 V. **The `5V` pin is never wired to anything on
  the carrier.**
- OLED: SSD1306 driving a 72 × 40 panel with a column offset; some libraries name the same panel
  "70 × 42". **Scanned 4 September: `0x3C` on SDA = GPIO5, SCL = GPIO6**, nothing else on the bus
  of the bare module. Whether the OLED has its own pull-ups is a meter reading still to take.
- Strapping pins **GPIO2, GPIO8, GPIO9** stay unused as inputs. GPIO8 drives the on-board LED,
  GPIO9 is the BOOT button — the display page button in firmware, nothing on the carrier.
- **Pin map, photo-verified 4 September** (USB on the left, OLED up). Row A, the side with the
  `PWR` and `IO8` LEDs, from the USB end: `5V GD 3V RX TX 2 1 0`. Row B, from the USB end:
  `10 9 8 7 6 5 4 3`. `RX`/`TX` are GPIO20/21. This is not the plain SuperMini's map.
- GPIO assignment: GPIO5/6 I²C (row B); **GPIO3** radar `OT2` (presence, row B far end);
  the board's marked **`RX` (GPIO20)** ← radar `OT1` (its TX) and **`TX` (GPIO21)** → radar
  `RX`, i.e. UART0, `Serial0` in the Arduino core (row A); **GPIO4** 1-Wire (row B). GPIO0 and
  GPIO1, two of the five ADC pins, stay free. Changed 5 September on the operator's question:
  the earlier choice of GPIO0/1 as a second UART avoided the ROM boot log that the C3 prints on
  `TX` at every reset, but that log cannot form a radar command (header `FD FC FB FA` plus a
  valid length), the USB console is native USB on this board so UART0 is otherwise unused, and
  the labelled pins are the ones a builder reaches for.
- Known SuperMini trait: the ceramic antenna couples badly and Wi-Fi drops at full TX power.
  Firmware limits TX power to about 8.5 dBm at start-up as part of the product.
- Pin rows: 8 pins each, **7 pitches (17.78 mm) apart — dry-fit confirmed by the operator on
  4 September**: pushed into the 14 × 20 board the module fits with three free columns on each
  side, i.e. rows at columns 4 and 11.
- Chip on the first unit: ESP32-C3 rev 4, 4 MB flash, MAC `E4:EC:E6:92:05:D4`.

## Sensor set, bus map and headers

| Device | Address / pin | Header | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SHT41 | `0x44` | `J_SHT` 4-pin: VIN, GND, SCL, SDA | Photo-confirmed order. The `SHT4X` board carries a 662K LDO, a level shifter and a fitted `103` array on the sensor side; its host-side pull-up pads are empty, so it adds **no** pull-up to the bus |
| VEML7700 | `0x10` | `J_VEML` 5-pin: VIN, 3VO (NC), GND, SCL, SDA | Photo-confirmed order (`HW-900`); same LDO-plus-shifter architecture with all four array elements in use, so about 10 kΩ per line; `3VO` never wired |
| HLK-LD2410S | `OT2` → GPIO3, `OT1` → `RX` (GPIO20), `RX` ← `TX` (GPIO21) | `J_RAD` 5-pin in the manual's J2 order: **3V3, GND, OT1, RX, OT2** | Datasheet- and photo-confirmed 3.3 V part. `OT2` is the presence output (high = occupied), direct to GPIO3. `OT1` is the UART **TX** despite the name. `C_RAD_BULK` 100 µF at the header for the ~118 mA transmit peaks |
| OLED on the SuperMini | `0x3C` | on board | Scan-confirmed on GPIO5/6 |
| DS18B20 probe | 1-Wire, GPIO4 | `J_1W` 3-pole terminal: 3V3, DQ, GND with `R_1W` 4.7 kΩ to 3V3 | Surface, water or outside-the-window temperature on the 2.5 m lead |

No address collides. Carrier pull-ups `R_PU_SDA`/`R_PU_SCL` are **4.7 kΩ**; with the VEML7700's
10 kΩ the line sees about 3.2 kΩ, with an assumed 10 kΩ on the OLED about 2.4 kΩ — 1.4 mA sink,
well inside the 3 mA limit. Bus speed 100 kHz; the bus never leaves the board.

Radar placement rules, from Room v2's lesson in reverse: the antenna face looks into the room
with no metal or copper in front of it, the module stands on its header at the board edge, and it
must not look across a fan, a curtain in a draught or a pet's bed — a 24 GHz radar reports every
one of those as a moving target. Presence is confirmed with a person sitting still and with the
room empty before it drives anything.

## Board layout — 14 × 20 holes (35.6 × 50.8 mm)

Columns 1–14 left to right, rows 1–20 top to bottom, viewed from the component side, USB
overhanging the row-1 edge. The 10 × 24 board does not fit: with the rows 7 pitches apart the
module needs 8 of the 10 columns and leaves no keep-out beside the antenna end.

**The authoritative hole-by-hole drawing is in
[`hardware/kicad/atmosmesh-spot/wiring.md`](../../hardware/kicad/atmosmesh-spot/wiring.md)
(section 3) and its SVGs.** In outline:

| Rows | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| 3–10 | `U1` socket rows at columns 4 (row A: `5V GD 3V RX TX 2 1 0`) and 11 (row B: `10 9 8 7 6 5 4 3`), 7 pitches apart |
| 9–13 | keep-out around the module's far end: buttons and antenna. No copper, no wire, no coating |
| 14 | `R_PU_SDA`, `R_PU_SCL` 4.7 kΩ, `C1` |
| 15 | `J_SHT` (cols 1–4), `J_VEML` (cols 7–11), both modules standing upright |
| 16–18 | decoupling, test points, `R_1W`, `C_RAD_BULK`/`C_RAD` |
| 20 | `J_RAD` (cols 1–5, radar upright, face outward), `J_1W` terminal (cols 8/10/12) |

`J_VEML` and `J_RAD` sit on different edges so the light sensor can face one way and the radar
the other; on a windowsill unit the radar faces the room and the VEML7700 faces up.

Wiring by pin name, all on one 3.3 V rail:

```text
U1 3V3 ──┬── J_SHT VIN  ──┤C_SHT 100n── GND
         ├── J_VEML VIN ──┤C_VEML 100n── GND          J_VEML 3VO: not connected
         ├── J_RAD 3V3  ──┬┤C_RAD_BULK 100µ (+)── GND
         │                └┤C_RAD 100n── GND
         ├── J_1W 3V3 ── R_1W 4k7 ── J_1W DQ ── U1 GPIO4
         ├── C1 100n ── GND
         └── R_PU_SDA 4k7 ── SDA      R_PU_SCL 4k7 ── SCL

U1 GPIO5 (SDA) ── SDA ── J_SHT SDA ── J_VEML SDA
U1 GPIO6 (SCL) ── SCL ── J_SHT SCL ── J_VEML SCL
J_RAD OT2 ── U1 GPIO3           presence, high = occupied, 3.3 V logic, direct
J_RAD OT1 ── U1 RX (GPIO20)     radar UART TX → frames in (C3 UART0 RX, Serial0)
J_RAD RX  ── U1 TX (GPIO21)     configuration out (C3 UART0 TX)
U1 GND ── ground rail ── every GND
U1 5V  ── nothing
```

## Power

USB only. The SHT41 and VEML7700 draw about a milliampere each, the OLED a few. The radar's
transmit bursts are why `C_RAD_BULK` sits at its header. The other real load is the C3's Wi-Fi
transmit burst (up to ~350 mA) on the SuperMini's small LDO, which is why the TX-power limit
matters for supply as well as for range. If the board browns out on connect, add 10 µF at
`TP_3V3` and record it.

## Firmware

- New composition root `firmware/src/products/atmosmesh_spot.cpp` per ADR-0001, new PlatformIO
  environment for the C3 (USB CDC on boot). The build chain is proven by
  `firmware/tools/c3scan`; the product repeats the hello with its name and station id.
- Reuse the I²C scan-and-report behaviour and the MQTT contract shape (`{value, unit, valid,
  age_ms}`, `id`, `device`). Station ids from `atmosmesh-0004` up, one per unit.
- Entities: `temperature`, `humidity` (SHT41), `lux`, `presence` (radar `OT2`, with a hold
  time), `temperature_probe` (DS18B20, unavailable when no probe answers). Optional `distance`
  from the LD2410S UART: the default minimal frame is five bytes (`6E`, state, distance cm,
  `62`) at 115200 8N1, a trivial decoder — but the product works on `OT2` alone first.
- OLED: pages rotated every few seconds, the BOOT button forces the next one; a presence glyph
  in the corner of every page. 72 × 40 holds one value legibly, not four.
- Bus speed 100 kHz. Wi-Fi TX power limited at start-up; RSSI logged with every publish.

## In scope

- One carrier; the first build goes on the bench next to Room for the comparison criteria.
- PlatformIO C3 product environment, host-tested Sensirion CRC, MQTT discovery for the new
  product, 1-Wire probe reading.

## Out of scope

- Battery operation. The SuperMini has no charger, and deep-sleep with an OLED and a radar is a
  different product.
- Any electronics outside besides the probe. Coating or potting the node is rejected above.
- A BME280, on the board or on a cable (withdrawn 5 September).
- The ESP32-C6 modules. Same spike, different chip; after the C3 works.
- Any 5 V sensor, including the LD2450. The board has no 5 V domain by design.
- Radar zones, distance thresholds and multi-target anything.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Given the PlatformIO C3 environment, when a firmware builds and flashes, then the serial
      log shows a hello and a bus scan on real hardware. **Met on 4 September by
      `firmware/tools/c3scan`** (see Evidence); the product firmware itself still has to repeat
      it with its name and station id.
- [x] Given the SuperMini photographed and scanned, when the OLED pins and the breakout pull-ups
      are recorded in `docs/hardware/inventory.md`, then the carrier's `R_PU_*` value is written
      down. **4.7 kΩ**; only the OLED's own pull-up is still a meter reading.
- [x] Given the LD2410S datasheet, when supply voltage, current and output level are recorded,
      then the radar header is either approved for 3.3 V or the radar is struck from this story.
      **Approved:** 3.0–3.6 V, all pins 0–3.3 V (`docs/hardware/datasheets/README.md`).
- [ ] Given the bus scanned with only the OLED fitted, then exactly `0x3C` answers; with the
      sensors fitted, `0x44` and `0x10` join it and nothing else.
- [ ] Given the node beside Room's SHT41 for one hour, when the readings are compared, then
      temperature agrees within 0.5 °C and humidity within 3 %RH, or the difference is explained.
- [ ] Given a person sitting still for 5 minutes, then `presence` stays `on`; given the room
      empty for 5 minutes with any fan or curtain in the radar's view noted, it is `off`.
- [ ] Given the probe rung out and connected, then `temperature_probe` tracks a hand and agrees
      with the SHT41 within 1 °C in still room air.
- [ ] Given Wi-Fi at limited TX power, when the node runs for 24 hours, then no reconnect gap
      longer than 5 minutes appears in the log.
- [ ] Given the probe unplugged at runtime, then the other entities keep updating and
      `temperature_probe` reports unavailable within one publish interval.

## Validation

- Automated: Sensirion CRC against datasheet examples; MQTT payload schema; unavailable-device
  behaviour for each sensor independently; presence hold-time logic.
- Manual: bus scans per stage, radar `OT2` idle and active levels with the meter before GPIO3 is
  connected, side-by-side comparison with Room, presence still-sitting and empty-room tests,
  RSSI log.
- Failure/edge case: any breakout unplugged at runtime; USB power cycled (reconnects without
  manual reset); radar `OT2` stuck high; probe lead shorted DQ to GND (the 4.7 kΩ limits it and
  the bus recovers when the short clears).
